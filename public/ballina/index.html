About SPL Ballina

Based in Ballina, we provide property and automotive locksmith services for residential and commercial clients across the Northern Rivers region, including Byron Bay and Lismore. Locked out or lost your keys? Our mobile locksmiths can come to you, and we repair and rekey existing car and property locks.

Our team also installs security systems for homes and offices, including alarms, intercoms, CCTV, access control and master key systems, and supplies products such as deadlocks, safes, transponder keys, remote access and architectural hardware. NSW Security Licence Number: 000102071.

Smart locks for Airbnb and holiday rentals

With a smart locking system you can host guests wherever you are. Access codes are sent to guests remotely, and you can monitor arrivals and departures and manage multiple properties and doors at once. Talk to our Ballina team about a smart lock for your holiday property, or read more about access control and keyless entry.
