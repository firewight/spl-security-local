Ajax StarterKit.
Start protected.

A clean starting point for a connected alarm: hub, motion detection, door protection and control fob in one expandable wireless system.

WHY AJAX

A security system that starts simple—and grows intelligently.

The StarterKit brings the essential layers together. Add the devices that suit your site later, from fire and flood detection to automation and video.

01

Four essentials

The kit combines an Ajax hub, MotionProtect motion detector, DoorProtect opening detector and SpaceControl key fob.

02

Fast, secure communication

Jeweller two-way radio communication uses encryption, frequency hopping and tamper protection between the hub and devices.

03

Room to expand

Build on the starter system with additional sensors, sirens, automation and compatible video devices as the property changes.

STARTERKIT AT A GLANCE

Alerted early.
In control anywhere.

StarterKit configurations and available connectivity can vary by market and chosen hub. SPL will confirm the right locally available kit and coverage plan before supply.
