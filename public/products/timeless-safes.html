LUXURY SAFES

Timeless Safes.
Protection with presence.

A Sentinel range for valuables that deserve more than storage: fire-rated protection, substantial construction and a refined interior option for collectors.

View specifications ›

WHY TIMELESS

Designed for what cannot be replaced.

The Timeless Series combines a luxury presentation with serious construction. Select a model, locking style and internal fit-out around the items you are protecting.

01

Rated protection

60-minute fire protection tested at 1700°F and a $50,000 cash rating in an unsupported application.

02

Strong by design

70 mm body walls, an 87 mm three-layer steel door and 32 mm locking bolts work together for substantial physical protection.

03

Made for collectors

Choose keyed, combination or digital locking, with luxury lining, lighting and optional watch-winder configurations.
