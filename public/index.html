Frequently asked questions

Is SPL available 24/7 for emergencies?

Yes. Our emergency locksmith and electronic security technicians are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call (07) 55 888 111.

How long has SPL been in business?

SPL was founded in 1990 as Surfers Paradise Locksmiths and has been securing the Gold Coast ever since. Today our team is close to 100 staff.

What areas do you service?

We service the Gold Coast from our Bundall office, Brisbane from our Gold Coast office, and Northern NSW from our Ballina showroom.

Are you licensed and accredited?

Yes. We hold QLD Security Licence 3247282 and NSW Security Licence 000102071, and we are members of the Master Locksmiths Association of Australia and the Australian Security Industry Association Limited (ASIAL member C908). All our locksmiths and technicians hold the correct QLD and NSW security licences.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes, free quotes are available. Call (07) 55 888 111 or use our contact form.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We give you a fixed price before we come out, so you know the cost upfront.

Do you offer business accounts?

Yes, we offer commercial accounts for businesses. Contact our team to set one up.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. All our work comes with a 12-month warranty on parts and labour.

Do you service or repair security systems installed by other companies?

Yes. We service, repair and maintain locks, alarm systems, CCTV and access control, including systems installed by other companies.

Do you cover areas outside the Gold Coast, Brisbane and Northern NSW?

Yes. We cover a wide area beyond the Gold Coast, Brisbane and Northern NSW. Get in touch to check we cover your area: call (07) 55 888 111 or use our contact form.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept EFTPOS, Mastercard and Visa.
