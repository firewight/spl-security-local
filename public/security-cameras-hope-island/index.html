Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the difference between wired and wireless security cameras?

    Wired security cameras require a direct connection to power and a recording device, offering stable performance, while wireless cameras use Wi-Fi for connectivity and are easier to install. Both have their advantages depending on your setup and preferences.

  • How long do security cameras store footage?

    The storage duration depends on the system and storage capacity. Many systems allow you to customise recording settings, with options ranging from a few days to several months, especially with cloud storage or large-capacity hard drives.
