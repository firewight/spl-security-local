Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can I use a safe to protect electronic devices like hard drives?

    Yes, many safes are designed to protect electronic devices and media from fire and water damage. Look for safes with a UL or similar rating for media protection to ensure your devices are properly safeguarded.

  • How do I choose the right size safe for my needs?

    The size of your safe should be based on what you plan to store, including future items you may add. It’s always a good idea to choose a safe slightly larger than you currently need to accommodate additional valuables over time.
