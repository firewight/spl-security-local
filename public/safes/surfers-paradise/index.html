Frequently Asked Questions

  • What features should I look for in a fireproof safe?

    A fireproof safe should offer a fire rating indicating how long it can protect contents in extreme heat, usually measured in hours. Look for models with solid construction, internal seals to protect against water damage and ample storage for your valuables and documents.

  • How do I maintain the locking system on my safe?

    To maintain your safe’s locking system, keep the locking mechanism clean and free of debris. Periodic professional servicing ensures smooth operation and addresses wear or potential malfunctions, especially for digital or combination locks.
