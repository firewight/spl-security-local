Expert Locksmiths & Security Services

Across our extensive network of locations, from the streets of Surfers Paradise to the serenity of Kingscliff, SPL Security Solutions is your premier partner for comprehensive locksmith and security services. Whether you're looking to upgrade your home security, safeguard your commercial assets or secure sensitive data, our experts are here to assist you throughout Brisbane, the Gold Coast and Tweed Heads with:

  • Locksmith Services: From key cutting and lock repair to emergency re-entry and restricted key systems, we provide a comprehensive range of residential, commercial and automotive locksmith services, 24/7. We also supply hardware for locks, doors and fire doors.
  • Security Systems:  Our integrated security solutions include CCTV cameras, alarm systems, access control and intercoms to safeguard your property.
  • Safe Supply, Installation & Maintenance: Ensure your most valuable items are secure with our selection of safes in various sizes and locking mechanisms. We also offer safe delivery, installation, repair and unlocking services.
