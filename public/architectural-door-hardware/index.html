Modern & Classic Designs

At SPL Security Solutions, we can source a selection of architectural door hardware designed to combine functionality and style. Whether you need durable fire door hardware, smooth-operating door closers or elegant handles, our styles cater to both modern and traditional designs.

We provide tailored solutions for a wide range of industries, including schools, hospitals, luxury hotels, and new construction projects.

Our streamlined process ensures a hassle-free experience:

  • Product Selection: Choose from trusted brands such as Dormakaba and Novas, known for their quality and reliability.
  • Scheduling: We’ll calculate the number of doors and required hardware, creating a detailed schedule tailored to your project.
  • Quote Preparation: Receive a comprehensive quote based on your selections.
  • Packaging & Delivery: Once confirmed, your products are carefully packaged and delivered directly to your site.

Whether you’re selecting door closers, handles, or custom products, our team is here to assist with special orders and unique requirements.

From commercial spaces to residential projects, we supply hardware that enhances the safety, usability and aesthetic appeal of your doors.
