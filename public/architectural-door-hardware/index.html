Commercial door hardware

Mortice locks, door furniture, door closers, door seals, hinges and cabinetware for commercial and residential projects. Whether you are fitting out a new build or upgrading an existing site, we help you select hardware that balances durability, compliance and day-to-day usability — from elegant architectural finishes through to proven safety and access hardware.

Our supply range also covers hotel locking and fire door hardware, seals and functional access equipment suited to high-traffic commercial applications. Browse deeper product ranges for levers and handles, door closers and seals, fire door hardware and lock hardware.

Sliding & swinging door operators

Automatic door operators — swinging and sliding — for commercial and public-use environments such as hospitals, schools, NDIS residences, offices and other high-traffic sites, whether a new build or a refurbishment. We advise on operators that suit traffic flow, accessibility and the wider door schedule for the project, so openings work seamlessly day to day.

Commercial washroom hardware

Commercial washroom and sanitaryware including hand dryers, grab rails, towel dispensers, waste bins, mirrors and signage — specified to meet AS1428.1. Our team helps you align washroom hardware with accessibility requirements across the project, alongside the wider architectural door and cabinetware schedule.

Compliance & scheduling

Our expert knowledge and highly trained staff advise and schedule your project so it remains compliant with the NCC, Australian Standards and fire rating. We prepare door-by-door schedules so builders, architects and site teams know exactly what is specified for each opening — including planning, scheduling, monitoring and project control through to handover.

For fire-rated openings, see our fire door hardware range and compliance support. Where electronic security is also in scope, our electronic security division can assist with CCTV, access control, intercoms and alarms.

Tendering & installation

SPL Architectural commits to returning your tender on time. We provide tendering and take-off services for door hardware, hotel locking, sanitaryware and keying requirements, and will prepare a specification or schedule when you need one. Should you require value management, we work closely with your procurement and design teams to deliver a quality build befitting the project.

From early tender packages through site installation support, we stay with the project so handover stays on programme.

Customised packing & deliveries

All hardware is packaged in door sets, with locks and door furniture handed — part of our standard service. Labelled door packs are staged to suit your building programme so site deliveries arrive organised by door and stage rather than as a single unsorted shipment. Dedicated software allows variations to be managed easily as the build evolves.
