Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the difference between mechanical and electronic locks?

    Mechanical locks use physical mechanisms such as keys and pins to function, while electronic locks rely on electronic signals activated by keypads, biometrics or RFID to unlock. Electronic locks often offer additional features such as remote control and access logs.

  • How can I make my existing locks more secure without replacing them?

    To enhance the security of existing locks, consider installing a high-security strike plate with long screws that go into the studs of the door frame, adding door jammers or using security films on glass panels near doors.
