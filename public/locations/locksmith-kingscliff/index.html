Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does weather affect locks and how can they be protected?

    Extreme weather conditions such as cold, heat and humidity can affect the operation of locks by causing metal components to expand or contract. Not only that, introducing moisture to a lock can lead to rust or freezing. To protect locks from weather-related damage, it is recommended to use weather resistant locks for outdoor use, apply lubricants designed for locks and regularly clean and maintain the lock to ensure its durability and functionality.

  • Are there any specific types of locks recommended for bi-fold doors?

    For bi-fold doors, multi-point locking systems that engage at several points along the door frame are recommended for enhanced security. Additionally, using keyed alike or master key systems can provide both convenience and high security, allowing multiple locks to be operated with a single key while maintaining robust protection.
