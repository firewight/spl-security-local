Expert Tips To Feel Safe in Your Home

Talk to an expert ↗(07) 5588 8111
SPL Security Solutions • July 19, 2021

We want you to feel safe and secure, there’s nothing more important than feeling safe in your home, so how can we help you?

Do you have a safe in your home?

Purchasing a safe is a worthy investment for homeowners. Whether you’re storing cash, jewellery, or sentimental treasures, safes can offer peace of mind and most importantly, security. ⁠SPL offer many types of quality safes, locks and secure storage for home and business security, including delivery and installation.

Does your home have an intercom so you can see who is at the door or gate?

An intercom system, also known as a door phone, is a two-way standalone electronic communication and monitoring device used to transmit audio/video messages. The ability to speak and/or see the visitor before you open the door offers a huge sense of security to your home, especially for those who are elderly or live alone. ⁠

Does your home have an alarm system or CCTV System?

A CCTV system and Alarm System can provide the security you need to monitor your home when you’re not there. There are various types of home alarm systems, each with their own level of security. We supply and fit the lot, from basic burglar alarms and CCTV systems, through to high definition, 24/7 digital surveillance cameras.

Have you had your locks re-keyed?

Rekeying a lock means having a locksmith adjust and change the tumblers of the locks. This prevents old keys from working without having to change the handle or knob on the door. ⁠A re-key has two significant benefits: convenience and security. SPL can provide a full security audit of your home. We can service your window locks, sliding door locks, front door and garage, and more, so you can ensure your new home is well protected and secure.

Do you need to update your door hardware?

SPL has an exclusive range of door hardware available for any home renovation project you have in mind. We have access to some of the best European brands and high-quality commercial equipment.
