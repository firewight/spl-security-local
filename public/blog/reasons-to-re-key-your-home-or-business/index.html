Reasons To Re-Key Your Home Or Business

SPL Security Solutions • January 10, 2023

Re-keying your home or business is a great way to add extra security without having to install new locks. The process involves changing the tumblers of your existing locks so one key will no longer work, while another key can be used instead. In this post, we’ll look at some of the reasons to re-key your home or business—read on to learn more!

Protecting Your Valuables

One of the main reasons why you might want to re-key your home or business is to protect valuable items from theft. If anyone unauthorised has been able to access to your home or business without you knowing, re-keying is an excellent way to prevent them from entering again. By changing the tumblers in each lock, you can ensure any old keys will no longer work and only those with the new key will be able to enter.

Some of the reasons people might have keys to your property include:

Ex-Employees: If an employee leaves your company and they had access to certain areas of your property, it’s important that you re-key those doors as soon as possible. This ensures any old keys they have won’t work anymore, which gives you peace of mind knowing former staff won’t be able to access restricted areas.

Lost Keys: If you have lost your keys or they’ve been stolen, your security could be compromised. Losing your keys can be a frustrating and stressful experience, It can also affect the security of your home or business. If someone finds your lost keys, they may be able to gain access to your property.

New Home or Business: A recently purchased home or business may have many keys still in circulation with previous owners, tenants and employees. When you buy a new property, it is important to re-key to ensure the previous owner’s keys and their duplicates will no longer work. This is especially important if you do not know the previous owner or are unsure how many copies of the keys may be in circulation.

Preventing Lock Bumping

Lock bumping is a technique that is becoming increasingly common among thieves. It can be used to open most standard pin tumbler locks quickly and easily. A bump key is a special tool that is used to manipulate the pins inside the lock, allowing it to be opened without the correct key.

This can be a major concern for home and business owners as it means that even if you have locks on your doors and windows, they may not provide the level of protection you expect. If a thief has access to a bump key, they may be able to gain entry to your home or business without much effort.

One way to protect against lock bumping is to regularly re-key your locks and update them with more secure options. By replacing your old locks with newer, more secure models, you can make it harder for thieves to gain entry to your home or business.

In conclusion, re-keying your home or business is an important step in maintaining the security of your property. Whether you have lost your keys, are changing ownership, or are updating locks for security purposes, re-keying can provide the peace of mind and protection you need.
