Top 5 Security Tips for your Home or Business

Talk to an expert ↗(07) 5588 8111
AUSHost • September 28, 2017

There can be little doubt that the security of any property is very important. With burglars on the Gold Coast becoming more and more advanced in their ability to access a business or home, many people are now opting to use a security system, which enables the occupant to feel safe from the threat posed by unwanted intruders.

There are certain security providers, such as SPL Security, who offer services other than just the protection of your house or business. Some important security tips and suggestions are as follows:

Tip No. 1: Keep a Close Check on Your Locks

It is vital to keep a close check on all your locks when you are about to go somewhere, especially before going on a vacation or closing up for the weekend. The best way to stay safe from this issue is to be focused while locking your doors and windows. It is so easy to become distracted and forget the back door or toilet window.

If, however, the unwanted happens and you forget your keys somewhere or you are broken into and want to get your locks replaced immediately, then you need a security service provider such as SPL Security who are able to deliver a 24 hour locksmith service.

Tip No. 2: Security Cameras

Another great tip is to install surveillance cameras, such as CCTV cameras and alarms. You need to be aware of the technology that can be used for the security and protection of your property. Keep your premises secure by hiring a company which not only provides you with the best services, but also provide routine maintenance checks and serviced at desired intervals. SPL Security systems provide you with the right technology and the right services within a reasonable budget.

Tip No. 3: Choose the ‘Right’ Security Company

It is best to choose a security company which offers you free quotes for your house or business, on services such as, cctv systems, access control, intercoms, alarm systems and electronic security maintenance. They will keep a regular check on your cameras and the other technological tools that you are using. You can also ask the team of experts from SPL Security to give you some helpful tips and advice.

Tip No. 4: Keep Locks Up To Date

It is also beneficial to upgrade your locks periodically. In addition to this, if you lose your keys, instead of getting a similar key cut, change your locks to a more advanced locking system. This will improve your security and make it harder for thieves who may try to access your home or business.

Tip No. 5: Have Your Property Assessed

The locksmiths at SPL will be happy to inspect your property so that you are aware of any additional security measures you may require. This would give you peace of mind but also have the added benefit of possible preventing you from any future burglary attempts.
