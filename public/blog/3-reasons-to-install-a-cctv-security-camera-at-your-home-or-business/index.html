3 Reasons to install a CCTV Security Camera at your Home or Business

Talk to an expert ↗(07) 5588 8111
AUSHost • November 4, 2017

People are now beginning to taking more ownership over the protection their families, business and property. With a SPL Security closed circuit television (CCTV) security camera, you will have the peace of mind that your loved ones and property are being watched over 24/7.

In an interview discussing the Queensland Police Service’s Statistical Review, Acting Chief Superintendent Hanlon urged the community to be vigilant with the security to their houses, vehicles and other property items, with the rate of offences against property increasing by 19.1 percent in the last year.

“A lot of break-ins and property thefts are opportunistic and are preventable,” Acting Chief Superintendent Hanlon said.

“We urge the community to use all available security options on their property to prevent opportunistic thefts as a result of unlocked or unsecured property.

Security

Security cameras and CCTV systems were invented to protect banks and casinos from theft, nowadays innovation in technology and access to high quality WiFi means they are now simple and inexpensive enough to be used in business premises and homes for everyday surveillance. With an SPL CCTV Security Camera you are able to monitor multiple rooms in your home or business from the one device. There are a range of security extras available such as night vision, HD camera, video and audio and outdoor security. Having a visual security camera also deters burglars so they are less likely to break into your home or business.

Safety

As well as a deterrent to burglars, with visual SPL security cameras the chances of being assaulted outside or in your home decreases substantially as the perpetrator does not want their actions recorded. You can also safety view who is at the front door before opening it. In business applications it can be used as a safety monitor, allowing viewing of potential hazard which can lead to costly OH&S issues. SPL Security’s CCTV cameras can also be used to monitor staff behaviours whether that relates to theft, punctuality, or productivity hence allowing you to safely make decisions with sound facts.

Protection

Having SPL cameras installed in strategic places comes in handy when you need to monitor actions and words of people or during an event. Modern security cameras & CCTV systems are not only equipped with high-quality video capabilities, but audio as well. The clear images coupled with flawless sound makes them more efficient than ever at recording a series of happenings. This is particularly helpful when dealing with a legal scenario, wherein the eye witness may have forgotten a certain important detail or may be providing with an accurate account of what really transpired. With a SPL security camera, you can provide evidence to the legal authorities so they can see the series of events as they really unfolded.
