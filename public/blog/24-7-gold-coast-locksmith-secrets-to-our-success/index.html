24/7 Gold Coast Locksmith Secrets To Our Success

Talk to an expert ↗(07) 5588 8111
SPL Security Solutions • February 19, 2019

When you call SPL Security, it’s often because you need help fast. And when you find yourself locked out, you know our mobile locksmith service will get to you fast.

Back in 1990, our founder Michael Valitutti would have saved the day for you from a van that he had purpose-built. Then, as now, a 24/7 Gold Coast locksmith at call is the key (pun intended) to our success.

We were known then as Surfers Paradise Locksmiths and when Michael expanded the business in 1998, the initials of that name helped create our new name, SPL Security. No longer identified for just Surfers Paradise and to bring about the next level of customer service and reliability, SPL took on employees and Michael took on a partner.

Big Expansion

In 2001, Michael and partners expanded further to become even more reliable and service more residents, businesses and motorists.

Now a leading name in Gold Coast locksmiths, the company has many different divisions including a security division, an electronics division, and an architectural hardware division, to name a few.

In 2004, SPL House in Ashmore Road, Bundall, came into being. This purpose-built premises was designed and constructed to serve Michael’s vision, the company’s commitment to its customers, and to guarantee the highest quality service and products.

Guiding Light

Michael continues to run the company with the same commitment and drive he had in 1990. As SPL’s Managing Director, he takes charge of all the company’s commercial operations.

The commercial division at SPL Security now has nearly 90 highly experienced, fully qualified members of staff.

Michael’s philosophy has never wavered. He insists “the customer’s perception is their reality” and this belief dictates the willingness to go above and beyond to achieve customer satisfaction from SPL’s products and services.

Our 24/7 Locksmiths

Michael Valitutti’s guarantee is that SPL will be with you in 1 hour or less—in the event of a burglary, or if you lose your keys, snap a key in the lock, or find your keys are locked inside your car.

SPL’s crack team of master locksmiths will come to you whatever time of day or night. You won’t need to wait for long in what can be distressing and stressful situations. Thanks to their experience with all types of locks, our caring team will have the problem rectified in no time.

When You Need Us

At SPL, every customer is a priority customer. Combined with hard work and dedication, SPL Security has earned its place as the best of the best Gold Coast locksmiths.

When you need a locksmith, any time, day or night, 365 days a year, you won’t be sorry to have our number listed in your phone’s contacts. Our contact page has all the details and more. Or simply push here to have the number dial for you now.
