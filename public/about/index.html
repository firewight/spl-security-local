Our Values

Founded in 1990 as Surfers Paradise Locksmiths, our company has evolved over the years into SPL Security Solutions, offering a diverse range of services and architectural hardware divisions.   

With a team of close to 100 proficient professionals, we are committed to upholding the belief that the customer’s perception is their reality, prioritising unparalleled service and premium products as our cornerstone principles, ensuring we consistently meet the standards of luxury and large-scale commercial projects.

Our Story

What began with a single van has grown into something far greater — a trusted name in complete security solutions.

At SPL Security, we know security is more than locks and hardware. It’s about protecting homes, businesses, people, and peace of mind. Built on hard work, integrity, and a commitment to doing the job properly, our reputation has been earned through consistent delivery, quality workmanship, and long-standing relationships with the communities we serve.

Today, with renewed energy and a clear vision for the future, under the guidance of managing director Kylie Wright, we combine practical experience with modern security solutions to deliver services that are responsive, dependable, and aligned to our clients’ needs.

From 24/7 locksmith services to comprehensive security outcomes across commercial, residential, and government environments, our focus remains the same: protect what matters most, deliver on what we promise, and stand behind our work.
