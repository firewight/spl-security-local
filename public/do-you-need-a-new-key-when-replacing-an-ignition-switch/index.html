Replacing an ignition switch can be a tricky and complicated task, and you’re probably wondering if you need to get a new key when replacing an ignition switch. The answer is – it depends on the vehicle model and the current condition of your key and lock cylinder.

Most vehicles will require a new key when replacing an ignition switch, as the old key has worn down and is incompatible with the new set. However, some cars may be able to use an existing key, depending on the condition and make of the key.

In this blog, we will discuss in more detail the factors to consider when replacing an ignition switch and whether or not a new key is necessary.

What Is An Ignition Switch And Why You Need To Replace It

An ignition switch is a part of the car’s electrical system that supplies power to certain components when you turn on the key. Over time, corrosion and wear can occur, which can lead to poor performance or even a vehicle that won’t start. That’s why it’s essential to replace your ignition switch every few years for optimal performance.

What Are The Signs Of A Faulty Ignition Switch?

If you’re having trouble with your car starting, it could be a sign that your ignition switch is faulty. Other symptoms can include the check engine light coming on, difficulty turning the key to start the car and strange noises when trying to start the vehicle.

What Do I Need For Replacing An Ignition Switch?

You will need a set of replacement ignition switch tools and the new ignition switch itself. Depending on the make and model of your car, you may also need a new key for the replacement to work correctly.

Do I Need A New Key When Replacing An Ignition Switch?

As stated previously, it depends on the make and model of your car. If you have an older model that uses a standard key, you may be able to get away with using the existing key when replacing the ignition switch. But if your vehicle has a specialised security system or a more complex electronic design, chances are you will need to get a new key for the replacement.

If you’re unsure whether or not you need a new key when replacing an ignition switch, it’s best to consult a professional locksmith who can advise you on the best course of action for your particular vehicle. At SPL Security Solutions, we can help you determine what type of lock and key you need for your ignition switch, as well as provide expert advice on how to install it properly.

Replacing an ignition switch can be tricky and getting the wrong type of lock and key can cost you a lot of time and money. That’s why it’s crucial to get the right advice from a professional locksmith before tackling this task.

Tips To Make Sure Your Replacement Ignition Switch Lasts Longer

Once your new ignition switch is installed, there are a few things you can do to ensure it lasts as long as possible. First and foremost, keep the area around the ignition switch clean and free of dust and debris. It’s also important to check your car’s electrical system regularly for any signs of wear or corrosion. Lastly, you should use only high-quality replacement parts when replacing an ignition switch.
