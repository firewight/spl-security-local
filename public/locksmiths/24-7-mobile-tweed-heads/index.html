Frequently Asked Questions

  • What should I consider when choosing a safe for my home or business?

    When selecting a safe, consider factors like size, type (wall, floor, or digital safe), and what you intend to store. It’s also important to choose a safe with appropriate security features, such as fire or tamper resistance, and ensure it’s professionally installed for maximum protection.

  • How does a master key system work?

    A master key system allows one key to open multiple locks while individual keys operate only specific locks. This is ideal for businesses or large properties, as it provides controlled access for authorised individuals while simplifying key management.
