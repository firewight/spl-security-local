Frequently Asked Questions

  • What should I do if my lock breaks unexpectedly?

    If your lock breaks, avoid forcing it as this may cause further damage. Contact a locksmith immediately to assess the issue and either repair or replace the lock.

  • Can a locksmith upgrade my current locks to smart locks?

    Yes, locksmiths can replace traditional locks with smart locks. They ensure the new system is installed correctly and provide guidance on its operation for enhanced security and convenience.
