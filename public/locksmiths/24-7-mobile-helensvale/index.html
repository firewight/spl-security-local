Frequently Asked Questions

  • What’s the difference between rekeying and replacing locks?

    Rekeying changes the internal mechanism of a lock so that old keys no longer work, while replacing involves installing a completely new lock. Rekeying is often more cost-effective and works well when the hardware is still functional.

  • Are mobile locksmiths equipped to work on high-security locks?

    Yes, mobile locksmiths are trained to handle high-security locks and carry specialised tools to work on them. Whether it’s installation, repair or rekeying, they can manage advanced locking systems efficiently.
