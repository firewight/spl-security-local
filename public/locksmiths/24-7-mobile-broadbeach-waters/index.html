Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do locksmiths handle modern smart locks during emergencies?

    Locksmiths are trained to troubleshoot and repair smart locks, ensuring they function correctly without damaging the system. Many can also reset or reprogram electronic locks on-site.

  • What should I do if I lose all keys to my home or vehicle?

    If all keys are lost, a locksmith can create new keys by rekeying the lock or using a key code. For vehicles, locksmiths can cut and program new keys, including transponder and remote keys.
