Frequently Asked Questions

  • What should I do if my key breaks inside the lock?

    If your key breaks inside the lock, avoid trying to extract it yourself as this could push the fragment further inside or damage the lock. Contact a locksmith who can safely remove the broken key and assess whether repairs or replacements are needed.

  • How secure are smart locks compared to traditional locks?

    Smart locks offer enhanced security with features like encrypted access, activity tracking, and the ability to change access codes remotely. When professionally installed, they provide a strong, reliable alternative to traditional locks while offering greater convenience.
