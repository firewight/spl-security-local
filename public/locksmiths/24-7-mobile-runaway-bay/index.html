Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are the benefits of installing a master key system for my business?

    A master key system allows you to control access across different areas of your business while reducing the number of keys you need. It provides convenience, improves security, and allows tiered access for staff members.

  • How often should I maintain or replace my locks?

    Locks should be inspected regularly for signs of wear, especially in high-traffic areas. Replacing or upgrading locks every 5–7 years, or sooner if they show signs of damage, ensures continued reliability and security.
