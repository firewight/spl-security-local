Service details

Residential Locksmiths

Our residential locksmith services are tailored to meet the security needs of homeowners. They include lock installation, repair and replacement, as well as key duplication and lockout services. Utilising tried and tested technologies, these services can significantly enhance the safety of homes.

Commercial Locksmiths

Our commercial locksmith services cater to businesses, offering custom solutions to protect assets and sensitive information. We supply and install everything from high-security lock systems and master key setups to access control, restricted key systems and safes.

Automotive Locksmiths

Our auto locksmith services provide solutions for vehicle-related lock and key issues. This includes cutting new keys, repairing and replacing locks and ignition systems, and providing emergency lockout services. Our mobile auto locksmiths are available 24/7.

Emergency Locksmiths

Our emergency locksmith services are available 24/7 to address urgent security problems. Whether you've locked yourself out, lost your keys or experienced a break-in, our team will respond promptly to resolve the issue.

Restricted Key Request

Restricted key systems offer a higher level of security through controlled access and keys that cannot be duplicated without authorisation. These systems are ideal for both residential and commercial properties.
