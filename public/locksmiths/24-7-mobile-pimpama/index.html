Frequently Asked Questions

  • What should I do if I lose all my car keys?

    If you lose all your car keys, contact a professional locksmith immediately. They can cut and program a replacement key, including transponder and smart key fobs, to get you back on the road.

  • How can I improve the security of my business without overhauling all locks?

    Rekeying your existing locks or implementing a master key system are cost-effective ways to improve security. Adding access control systems or upgrading to high-security locks can further enhance protection without significant changes.
