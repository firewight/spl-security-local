Frequently Asked Questions

  • How can I avoid getting locked out again?

    Keeping a spare key in a secure, accessible location is a simple way to avoid lockouts. Alternatively, consider upgrading to a keyless or smart lock system for added convenience and security.

  • What should I do if my car key won’t turn in the ignition?

    A key that won’t turn might indicate a worn-out key or a damaged ignition. Avoid forcing it, as this could cause further damage and contact a professional locksmith to resolve the issue safely.
