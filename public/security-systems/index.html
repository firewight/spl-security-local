Service details

Security Cameras & CCTV Systems

Our CCTV systems deliver continuous surveillance with multiple cameras connected to a central recording device for live viewing and playback. Our high-resolution security cameras provide real-time footage, acting as the eyes of your security, capturing essential moments.

Alarm Systems

Alarm systems act as a crucial deterrent against unauthorised entry, immediately alerting property owners and, in some cases, authorities of a breach. These electronic security systems can range from simple door sensors to more complex networks incorporating motion detectors.

Alarm Monitoring

Experience round-the-clock alarm monitoring with rapid response to potential security breaches, emergencies or suspicious activity. Our reliable service ensures immediate action and enhanced protection at all times.

Intercom Systems

Intercom systems enhance the control of access to a property by enabling voice and video communication between the exterior and interior. This allows occupants to verify the identity of visitors before granting access.

Access Control

Access control systems restrict and manage entry to a property, ensuring only authorised individuals can enter specific areas. These electronic security systems can be operated via keycards, fobs, biometrics or mobile credentials, providing a high level of security and flexibility.

Maintenance & Servicing

Regular maintenance and servicing are crucial for ensuring the effective operation of electronic security systems over time. This involves periodic checks and diagnostics to prevent failure and firmware updates to incorporate the latest security protocols.
