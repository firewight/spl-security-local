AT A GLANCE

Have you ever hesitated to invest in a home security system because you’re unsure where you’ll be living next year or month?

SPL Security Solutions · Published 30 April 2024

April 30, 2024

Have you ever hesitated to invest in a home security system because you’re unsure where you’ll be living next year or month? If you’re a renter, this concern is all too common. You need a security solution that moves as quickly as you do without the headache of long-term contracts or invasive installations. Fortunately, there are innovative, flexible options available that ensure your peace of mind can travel with you from one rental to the next. Here are five key solutions to consider for enhancing the security of your non-permanent living space.

1. Portable Security Cameras 

Start with something simple yet highly effective: portable security cameras. These devices offer flexibility to renters who need an easy setup and can take their investment with them when they move. Wireless models can be placed anywhere in your home without worrying about drilling holes or running wires. Look for cameras with features like remote viewing, motion detection and night vision to keep an eye on your home from anywhere at any time. 

2. Smart Door Locks 

Smart door locks provide both security and convenience, allowing you to unlock your door with a smartphone app, a unique code or even your fingerprint. Many models are easy to install on existing door hardware, making them ideal for renters. Plus, you can remove and take them to your next home, ensuring you’re always in control of who has access. These locks often feature real-time alerts and activity logs, keeping you informed about who enters and leaves your home, even when you’re away. 

3. Window Alarms 

Simple, affordable and effective window alarms are a must-have for renters. These small devices can be adhered directly to your windows and emit a loud alarm if the window is opened unexpectedly. This is a great way to deter potential intruders and alert you and your neighbours to unauthorised entry attempts. Additionally, their portability means you can easily remove and reinstall them at your next residence, maintaining security continuity across your moves. Perfect for all types of windows, they offer an added layer of protection without needing professional installation or modification to rental properties. 

4. Personal Security Devices 

Don’t overlook personal security devices such as portable door alarms or travel locks, which can be used at home and on the go. These devices are handy for securing ground-floor apartments or rooms that might be more accessible to outsiders. They require no installation and can be set up in seconds to provide extra security wherever you are. Additionally, their compact size and ease of use make them ideal for renters who need a quick security fix without compromising effectiveness. 

5. Renters Insurance 

While not a physical security device, renters insurance significantly affects your overall security strategy. It can protect your belongings from theft or damage and provide liability coverage if someone is injured in your rented space. Make sure your policy covers the replacement cost of your belongings and consider adding additional coverage for high-value items. Always review and update your policy annually to ensure it aligns with your current needs and the value of your possessions.
