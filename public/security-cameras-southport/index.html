Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do security cameras work in low-light conditions?

    Yes, most modern security cameras are equipped with night vision or low-light technology, allowing them to capture clear footage even in dim environments. Ensure the cameras you choose meet your specific lighting requirements.

  • How often should security cameras be maintained?

    It’s recommended to inspect and maintain security cameras every 6–12 months. Regular checks ensure the lenses are clean, software is updated and all components are functioning properly.
